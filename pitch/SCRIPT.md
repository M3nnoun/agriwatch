## 🛡️ Likely judge questions (have these ready)

| Question | Crisp answer |
|---|---|
| **How do you prevent fraud / false payouts?** | Parametric = no human claim to game. Payout depends only on independent satellite + weather data the farmer can't influence. |
| **Who funds the payouts?** | A reinsurance-backed pool; premiums (often subsidized by gov/NGO) fund expected losses, reinsurance covers the tail. |
| **What about basis risk** (data says fine but crop failed)? | We fuse satellite *and* weather to reduce it, and tune thresholds per crop with FAO data; payouts are a safety cushion, not full indemnity. |
| **Connectivity in rural areas?** | Works on SMS/USSD too, not just WhatsApp — any phone, low bandwidth. |
| **Why will farmers trust it?** | First payout builds trust; onboarding + alerts are in their language via a channel they already use daily. |
| **Accuracy of Sentinel-2 on tiny plots?** | 10 m pixels + weather fusion; for very small plots we aggregate to the village/zone level. |
| **Is the AI essential or a gimmick?** | It turns raw risk scores into trusted, localized, actionable guidance — the difference between data and a message a farmer acts on. |
